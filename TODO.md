## Break-even stop management

- [x] **Narrow-only stop moves:** once the market has travelled `breakEvenTriggerPoints` in favour of a trade Signal Desk opened, its stop is moved to entry plus `breakEvenOffsetPoints`. Distances are MetaTrader points aligned onto the symbol `tickSize` (a buy rounds down, a sell rounds up); a stop is never widened and a position already at least as protected is marked handled.
- [x] **Broker rules respected:** `stopsLevel` and `freezeLevel` are checked before any request, so a stop that is too close to the market is deferred and retried instead of rejected; the decision always uses a fresh synchronized quote and the live position from the terminal state, and fails closed on crossed or missing prices, a missing specification, or an unrecognized position direction.
- [x] **Persisted per position:** `break_even_moves` keeps one row per broker position (status `moved`, `already_beyond`, `closed`, `deferred`, `failed`, `unknown`, the previous and new stop, the quote used, attempt count, next retry, and the sanitized provider message). Terminal statuses are never retried; a deferral retries without consuming an attempt while a broker rejection retries up to ten times; `unknown` never retries because the broker may already have applied the change. `TRADE_RETCODE_NO_CHANGES` counts as protected, and the recorded `trades.stop_loss` follows the broker.
- [x] **Same loop, never in the way:** the sweep runs in the execution worker after an idle tick, and every fifth iteration while orders are being processed so a busy signal stream cannot starve protection, throttled by `BREAK_EVEN_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MS` (default 5000 ms, floor 1000). Auto-Trading off, an account change, an undeployed account, or the runtime switch all stop the request before it reaches MetaAPI; only outcomes the broker actually answered consume an attempt; changing a distance re-arms handled positions.
- [x] **MetaAPI surface widened once:** the trading adapter now calls the SDK's `modifyPosition(positionId, stopLoss)` only (no take profit is ever sent, so the existing take profit survives) and keeps the provisioning/user token server-side. No close, partial close, or pending-order capability was added.
- [x] **Settings and UI:** `GET/PUT /api/settings` carries `breakEvenEnabled`, `breakEvenTriggerPoints`, and `breakEvenOffsetPoints` with server-side bounds; Risk settings gains a Break Even section that states the scope and that Auto-Trading off also stops these moves, and each trade row shows its break-even outcome and reason. The required live-trading warning text is unchanged.

**Break-even validation:** backend suite passes 150/150, with 27 new tests for the decision function (trigger, both sides, tick alignment, never-widen, stops/freeze deferral, fail-closed inputs), failure classification (deferred vs rejected vs ambiguous), and the sweep (runtime gate, throttle, re-check before the write, closed positions, `NO_CHANGES`, provider rejection sanitized without the token, unsynchronized account, worker ordering, and a failing sweep not taking the loop down). The frontend gained a render-based component test suite (`npm test` in `client/`, 5/5) and the production build succeeds. No live MetaAPI request was made; the broker adapter is faked in every test.

## Plan pricing, free-tier enforcement, and idle-account deletion

- [x] **Free is review-only, enforced in two places:** Free carries `maxTradingAccounts: 0`, so `POST /api/metaapi/provisioned-accounts` answers `402` (`PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED`, `upgradeRequired: true`) rather than letting a free workspace connect an account it should not trade on. Because a count gate cannot reach a link that already exists, the execution engine additionally refuses to place an order — and break-even candidates are filtered — unless the user is on `active` or `non_renewing`, and `PUT /api/settings` will not accept an explicit Auto-Trading or Break-even enable without a paid plan. Turning either switch off, and every other setting, always stays available.
- [x] **Zero is a limit, not "unset":** the effective-limit merge accepts `0`, the deployed tier now outranks the `plan_limits` snapshot bought earlier (a snapshot only carries a plan key the catalogue no longer has), and a zero allowance is announced as "The Free plan does not include connected trading accounts" instead of "allows 0". The Billing page renders the free card as review-only and offers the next tier up rather than always Pro/Premium.
- [x] **30-day inactivity deletion:** `users.last_active_at` is refreshed on sign-in, on a settings save, and after a signal executes. `server/services/inactivity.js` sweeps at startup and every six hours (`INACTIVITY_WARNING_DAYS`/`INACTIVITY_DELETION_DAYS`/`INACTIVITY_SWEEP_LIMIT`, `INACTIVITY_CLEANUP_ENABLED=false` to stop it): day 25 records a countdown warning notice, day 30 deletes the account from MetaAPI, removes the `metaapi_accounts` link, and turns Auto-Trading off when that account was the selected execution account. Existing accounts stay deployed and are grandfathered for review.
- [x] **Deletion that cannot strand a trade or lose a retry:** paying subscribers and admins are exempt (both in the candidate query and in the decision, so an exempt account never occupies a sweep slot or triggers a provider read); an account whose synchronized state still shows a position or pending order is deferred with the reason recorded; an unreadable position state fails closed; a MetaAPI failure keeps the local row so a later sweep retries, and ten failed attempts mark the account `failed` with a manual-action notice. Every account is handled in its own `try`, so the job never dies, and nothing is deleted when the provider did not confirm it. Accounts are never slept, woken, or undeployed.
- [x] **Visible, not emailed:** `GET /api/metaapi/linked-accounts` returns each account's idle age and warning state plus the workspace policy, `account_activity_notices` (RLS-enabled, user-scoped) holds the history, Trading Accounts shows a day-25 banner with the days remaining, and the add-account form states the policy. This project has no email transport, so `warningText()` is the single seam a later sender reuses.
